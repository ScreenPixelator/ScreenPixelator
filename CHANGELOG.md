# Changelog

## 1.1
- Quick choices (presets) that also set faces, darkness, strength and block size: Hide nudity only, Women covered (men visible), Men covered (women visible), Everyone covered, Women fully covered (eyes only, 85% dark, 6 px blocks), Anonymous (eyes hidden). Choosing one applies it at once.
- Buttocks are covered in the women, men and women-fully-covered choices; with *Men covered, women visible*, buttocks of a recognised woman stay visible.
- No more *Save and apply* button: every change takes effect by itself, 2 seconds after the last change.
- *Hide nudity only* is the default for new installations, with milder strength (catch more nudity 80, people 65).
- Smaller zones around breasts and buttocks (margin 20%, 10% for *Women fully covered*); genitals keep at least 50%.
- Smoother outlines with small blocks, and rounded instead of square zones around body parts.
- *Hide only the eyes* uses a wider band over the eyes.
- Default block size 15 px. The darkness warning starts at 80%.
- No more pink square flashing in the top left corner at every start.
- Uninstalling no longer waits for you to click OK on a "removed" message.
- The settings window no longer hangs or opens as an empty little window; errors are logged in `settings.log`.
